About Feihe

Small Screens. Serious Engineering.

For over fifteen years, we've focused on one thing: making compact displays that big brands treat as an afterthought.

Who We Are

Feihe Display Technology was founded in Shenzhen in 2011 with a simple observation: everyone was fighting over the 55-inch living room, and almost nobody was making the 20-inch bedroom, kitchen, hotel or workshop screen genuinely good.

Today we design, assemble and quality-check every unit in-house — smart TVs, digital TVs and monitors from 13 to 27 inches — shipping more than 1.2 million units a year to retailers, distributors, hospitality groups and individual buyers across the United States and Europe.

We stay deliberately niche. Because we only build small-format displays, our tooling, firmware, supply chain and QC processes are tuned for exactly these panel sizes. That's how a 24-inch smart TV from Feihe can outperform a stripped-down big-brand model at the same price point.

Our Story

Milestones

2011

Founded in Shenzhen

Feihe starts as a small OEM assembler for portable TVs, building a reputation for tight QC in a crowded market.

2014

First in-house brand line

We launch our own compact TV range for European retailers, adding DVB-T2 tuners and CE-certified tooling.

2017

Smart TV platform

Android-based smart TVs enter the lineup, bringing streaming apps to small screens in hotels and homes.

2020

US market entry

FCC and UL certifications open the door to US distributors; annual shipments pass 800,000 units.

2023

EU fulfillment hub

A central European warehouse starts stocking best-selling models for 2–4 day delivery.

2026

1.2M units and counting

The 27″ 4K and QHD Pro monitors launch, extending the lineup into premium small-format displays.
