Frequently Asked Questions

Does the 24″ smart TV support Netflix and YouTube?

Yes. The TV runs Google TV with the Google Play Store pre-installed, so Netflix, YouTube, Disney+ and thousands of other apps are one click away. Some apps require your own subscription.

Can I wall-mount this TV?

Yes. The FH-24STV has a standard VESA 100×100 mm mount pattern on the back and weighs just 3.4 kg without the stand, so any VESA-compatible bracket up to 32 inches will fit.

Does it work in Europe and the US?

The unit ships with a multi-region power adapter (100–240 V) and dual-band Wi-Fi. EU units include a DVB-T2/T/C tuner variant; US units include an ATSC tuner variant. Choose the region at checkout or contact sales.

Does the USB-C port charge my laptop?

Yes. The USB-C port supports DisplayPort Alt Mode with 65 W power delivery, so most 13–14 inch laptops run and charge from a single cable. Larger gaming laptops may need their own charger.

Is this monitor good for photo editing?

With 99% sRGB coverage and a factory calibration target, the FH-27M4K is well suited for web-standard photo and design work. It covers sRGB, not wide-gamut Adobe RGB or DCI-P3.

What is the difference between this and the 27″ QHD Pro model?

The FH-27M4K offers 4K resolution with 65 W USB-C; the FH-27MQHD offers QHD with a fully calibrated panel (ΔE < 2), 90 W USB-C and a pivot stand. Choose 4K for media and detail work, QHD Pro for color-critical design.
