Frequently Asked Questions

Does the 24″ smart TV support Netflix and YouTube?

Yes. The TV runs Google TV with the Google Play Store pre-installed, so Netflix, YouTube, Disney+ and thousands of other apps are one click away. Some apps require your own subscription.

Can I wall-mount this TV?

Yes. The FH-24STV has a standard VESA 100×100 mm mount pattern on the back and weighs just 3.4 kg without the stand, so any VESA-compatible bracket up to 32 inches will fit.

Does it work in Europe and the US?

The unit ships with a multi-region power adapter (100–240 V) and dual-band Wi-Fi. EU units include a DVB-T2/T/C tuner variant; US units include an ATSC tuner variant. Choose the region at checkout or contact sales.

Will this work on 12 V power in a camper or boat?

Yes. The FH-195STV accepts a 12 V DC input directly, so it runs from car batteries, solar setups and marine systems with the included adapter or any regulated 12 V source.

Which streaming apps are supported?

YouTube and Prime Video come pre-installed, and the Android 11 app store gives access to thousands more. Availability of specific apps can vary by region and account.

Is 1366×768 resolution enough?

For a screen this size — typically watched from 1–2 meters — HD resolution looks clean and keeps power draw low. If you want Full HD, see the 22″ or 24″ models.
