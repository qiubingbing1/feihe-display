Feihe Launches 27″ 4K UHD Monitor Across Europe

Feihe today announced the European availability of the FH-27M4K, a 27-inch 4K UHD monitor that brings genuine 3840 × 2160 resolution, 99% sRGB color coverage and USB-C power delivery to a price point normally reserved for 1080p panels.

Stock has already landed in our central EU fulfillment hub, so orders placed this week ship within 48 hours to addresses across the European Union, the UK and Switzerland. The launch price is $219 / €229.

Built for Work From Anywhere

The FH-27M4K was designed around a simple problem: laptop users want a big, sharp screen without a nest of cables. The USB-C port carries video, data and up to 65 watts of charging power over a single cable, so a modern laptop connects with one plug. HDMI 2.0 and DisplayPort 1.2 inputs keep desktops, consoles and older machines welcome too.

  • 3840 × 2160 IPS panel, 350 nits brightness
  • 99% sRGB coverage, 8-bit + FRC (1.07 billion colors)
  • Height-adjustable stand with tilt and swivel; VESA 100 compatible
  • CE, RoHS and ENERGY STAR certified for the European market

Availability

The FH-27M4K is available now from the Feihe online store at $219, and through our distribution partners across the EU. B2B customers ordering 50 units or more should request a project quote for volume pricing and custom packaging options.

"We kept hearing the same complaint from small-office buyers: 4K monitors were either too expensive or too big for their desks. The FH-27M4K answers both — full 4K in a 27-inch frame at a 1080p price."

— Feihe Product Team
