How to Choose a Small Smart TV for Kitchen & Bedroom

Big-screen buying advice is everywhere. Small-screen advice — the TV that actually goes in your kitchen, bedroom, dorm or camper — is strangely scarce. Here's the short version of what matters, based on fifteen years of building screens under 27 inches.

1. Match the Screen to the Distance, Not the Room

The rule of thumb: your viewing distance in feet should be roughly double the diagonal in inches. A 19.5-inch TV is perfect from 3–4 feet — a kitchen counter or the foot of a dorm bed. A 22–24 inch screen suits 4–6 feet, which covers most bedrooms. Beyond that distance, consider a 27-inch model instead.

2. HD or Full HD?

Under 20 inches viewed at close range, HD (1366 × 768) looks clean and keeps power draw low. From 20 inches up, Full HD (1920 × 1080) makes menu text, captions and interface elements visibly crisper. If the TV doubles as a monitor for a laptop, go Full HD — always.

3. The Smart Platform Matters More Than the Panel

On a 24-inch TV, panel differences are subtle; the software experience is not. A TV running Google TV or Android 11 gets you the full app store, regular updates and casting. Avoid closed-platform "smart" TVs that only offer a handful of fixed apps — you'll outgrow them in a year.

4. Check the Mounting and Inputs

Kitchen and bedroom installs usually mean wall mounting. Confirm the TV has a standard VESA pattern (75 × 75 mm or 100 × 100 mm for this size class) before buying a bracket. Count your inputs too: one HDMI for a console, one for a streaming stick or set-top box, plus a USB port for media playback covers most households.

5. One Question for Campers and Boats

If the TV might live on wheels or water, check for a 12 V DC input. Models like our 19.5-inch smart TV run directly from vehicle and solar power — no inverter, no fuss.
