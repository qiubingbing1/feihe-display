Feihe Expands EU Warehouse for 2–4 Day Delivery

Feihe has opened an expanded central European fulfillment hub, tripling local stock of our six best-selling models. Orders placed before 15:00 CET now ship the same working day and arrive in 2–4 business days across the EU, UK and Switzerland.

What's Stocked

The hub keeps standing inventory of every core product, including the 24″ Smart TV with Google TV, the 22″ Digital TV with triple DVB tuners, the 24″ 100Hz monitor and the newly launched 27″ 4K UHD monitor. Retail partners can request reserved stock allocations for seasonal peaks.

Why It Matters for B2B Buyers

Until now, European wholesale orders shipped from Shenzhen with 25–35 day ocean transit. Local stock changes that math completely: replenishment orders land in days rather than weeks, retail partners carry less safety stock, and warranty swaps can be handled from within the EU without a round trip to Asia.

"Our retail customers used to plan their ordering around our shipping schedule. Now we plan around theirs. That's what a real distribution partnership should feel like."

— Feihe EU Operations

Customs & Compliance

All units in the EU hub are imported with duty paid and carry CE and RoHS documentation, so business customers can resell immediately without additional certification steps. US customers continue to be served from our Delaware facility with 1–3 day domestic delivery.
